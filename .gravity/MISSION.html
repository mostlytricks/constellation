01Mission

Make building a presentation a composition problem, not a blank-page problem. A deck is stars arranged into a picture: components are the stars, patterns the lines between them, the design theme the sky — and the storyline is the picture the constellation draws. The five skills decompose decks you admire into those parts and recombine them with a new idea into a finished .pptx that argues.

north star: a pitch you would actually give, composed in minutes from patterns your own decks earned
first wedge: consultant-mood pitch decks (EN/KR) through the deck-spec seam
later shape: a pattern library that compounds — every deck analyzed makes the next one cheaper
Staged, not confused. Synthetic fixtures staged the whole machine first so real-deck evidence lands on working rails — the fixtures are scaffolding, never the product.

02Why This First

The hardest problems here are structural, not visual: an honest intermediate representation (the seam), a privacy wall a public repo can trust, and extraction that sees what PowerPoint actually stores (themes, inheritance, bullet XML). Consultant pitches force all three early — heavy theme inheritance, exhibits, and titles that must argue.

StagePurpose
NowMachine proven on synthetic evidence — five skills, seam v1, two theme families, gates green.
NextReal-deck evidence: earned templates and themes; the seam revised to v2 by what decks actually contain.

03Product Principles

  • Evidence over invention. Nothing enters the library that wasn't extracted from a deck; schema changes wait for the deck that demands them; unknowns stay visible OPEN: lines, never plausible filler.
  • Facts by script, judgment by agent. extract.py and build.py are deterministic and never interpret; archetypes, briefs, storylines, and specs are agent judgment. The boundary is never blurred.
  • The privacy wall. This repo is public: real decks and their text are never committed — only text-stripped generalizations graduate to the library.
  • A deck argues before it decorates. Titles are claims the slide must prove; the title spine alone must carry the case (the storyline skill's ghost-deck test).
  • Generate from template, never edit in place. .pptx round-tripping drops fidelity; composition is always forward from spec.

04Architecture Principle

Everything reduces to one seam: the deck-spec — the JSON contract between understanding decks (analyze/templatize) and producing them (compose). Skills may grow, templates may multiply, but they meet only at the seam; a capability that bypasses it (hand-drawn geometry, in-place edits) is off-mission. The system in one domain:

DomainPrinciple (the why)
deck-specOne honest, versioned interface — build.py is its only validator, and it refuses what it cannot faithfully render.

06Current Non-Goals

  • No in-place deck editing. We generate from templates; a WYSIWYG round-trip editor is a different product and breaks the fidelity principle.
  • No invented design. Themes come from analyzed evidence, not taste — the design guide is extracted, never imagined.
  • No speculative schema. Pictures, numbered lists, multi-master support wait for the real deck that demands them (SPEC OPEN list).
  • Not a service. This is a Claude Code skill set operated in-session — no server, no UI, no datastore beyond files in this repo.
